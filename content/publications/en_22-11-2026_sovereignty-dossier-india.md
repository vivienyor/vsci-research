## 2. Vector-by-Vector Structural Audit

### 🛡️ Vector C — Cyber & Cognitive Capability (`0.72` | Contribution: `0.1800`)
India maintains robust cyber defense institutions and demonstrates readiness for decisive administrative control over its network perimeter (including preemptive regional internet shutdowns and bans on adversarial Chinese applications). Concurrently, the country’s semantic space remains open to American digital platforms, which extract behavioral rent from the world’s largest user base.

### 🏭 Vector I — Physical & Energetic Infrastructure (`0.50` | Contribution: `0.1500`)
Commercial data center construction is booming, spurred by domestic data localization mandates. However, server fleets and cloud infrastructure remain predominantly operated by U.S. hyperscalers, while domestic semiconductor manufacturing under the *Make in India* initiative is still in its initial phase of establishing outsourced assembly and testing (OSAT) facilities and mature-node fabs.

### 💻 Vector S — Technological & Logical Stack (`0.45` | Contribution: `0.1350`)
India possesses an immense IT engineering workforce and has successfully deployed the state-backed **India Stack** (Aadhaar biometric identity, UPI instant payments). Nevertheless, across foundational system layers—operating systems, DBMS, and frontier AI models—the country remains a net importer of American technology.

### 📉 Vector V — Volatility & Shock Resilience (`0.42` | Contribution: `0.0870`)
Moderate volatility is anchored by the successful displacement of Western card networks (Visa/Mastercard) in domestic retail payments via the national **RuPay** network and **UPI** protocol, insulating domestic financial clearing from external cutoffs. Structural vulnerability persists due to reliance on imported energy and high-tech capital equipment.

## 3. Geotechnological Dependency Matrix & Network Friction ($\Delta VSCI$)

* **⬅️ INFLUENCES:** **Global South Contours (Nascent DPI Export)**.  
  Currently a net technology recipient at the foundational layer, India is actively promoting *India Stack* standards as an open-architecture alternative to Western and Chinese payment platforms across developing economies.
* **➡️ DEPENDS ON:** **United States**, **AWS**, **Microsoft**, **Google**.  
  Acute corporate and digital sector reliance on American enterprise software and hyperscale cloud compute.
* **🔄 COMPETES WITH:** **China**.  
  Intense geopolitical and geoeconomic rivalry for status as Asia’s premier manufacturing and tech hub, underscored by sweeping bans on Chinese software and telecom equipment.
* **🤝 COOPERATES WITH (Multi-Vector Balancing):** **United States**, **Russia**.  
  Executing a strategy of distributed resilience: deep IT talent and R&D partnership with Washington alongside enduring defense-technical, energy, and currency settlement ties with Moscow within BRICS.

## 4. Chokepoint Stress-Test (2026–2030 Scenario Horizon)

> **Scenario: "Asymmetric Geopolitical Alignment Pressure":**  
> External coercion forcing India to sever ties with a major geopolitical pole (e.g., secondary sanctions over Russian cooperation or border escalation with China) would disrupt telecom component supply chains. Long-term resilience hinges on meeting commissioning timelines for domestic semiconductor packaging and fabrication plants by 2028.
