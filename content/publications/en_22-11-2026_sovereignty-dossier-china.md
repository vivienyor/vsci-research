## 2. Vector-by-Vector Structural Audit

### 🛡️ Vector C — Cyber & Cognitive Capability (`0.95` | Contribution: `0.2375`)
The highest rating for cognitive-cybernetic insulation globally. Institutionalized PLA cyber warfare units combined with multi-layered cross-border traffic filtering grant China’s semantic sphere near-total immunity against external information operations and Western platform rent extraction.

### 🏭 Vector I — Physical & Energetic Infrastructure (`0.92` | Contribution: `0.2760`)
The Great Firewall (GFW) has transformed China’s domestic internet into an autonomous digital jurisdiction backed by sovereign root DNS mirrors. Foreign hyperscalers have been reduced to a statistical margin (<3% cloud market share), with infrastructural dominance held by national champions (**Alibaba Cloud**, **Tencent Cloud**, **Huawei Cloud**) supported by the world’s largest baseload power grid and rare-earth refining monopoly.

### 💻 Vector S — Technological & Logical Stack (`0.88` | Contribution: `0.2640`)
China has engineered an almost closed-loop hardware-software stack: spanning domestic chip design (**HiSilicon**) and mature/transitional node fabrication (**SMIC**) to comprehensive replacement of Western operating systems across government agencies with **Kylin** and **Deepin**, and mobile ecosystems with **HarmonyOS**. The domestic cognitive market is wholly served by sovereign foundation models (Ernie Bot, Qwen, Baichuan).

### 📉 Vector V — Volatility & Shock Resilience (`0.65` | Contribution: `0.0525`)
The sole vector holding China below the `0.85` threshold. Despite operating its own cross-border interbank payment system (**CIPS**) and rolling out the digital yuan (**e-CNY**), the PRC economy remains structurally exposed to maritime energy and raw material import chokepoints, alongside export controls targeting specialty chemicals and leading-edge photolithography tools.
