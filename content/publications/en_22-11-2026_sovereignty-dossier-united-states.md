## 2. Vector-by-Vector Structural Audit

### 💻 Vector S — Technological & Logical Stack (`0.98` | Contribution: `0.2940`)
The United States maintains an unprecedented monopoly across the upper and middle layers of the computational hierarchy. Control over client and server operating systems (Windows, macOS, enterprise Linux distributions), cloud hyperscalers (AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud), and core artificial intelligence architectures grants Washington global algorithmic dominance.

Within the logic of *Technological Westphalia*, the U.S. acts as the primary standard-setter and global rent-extractor: vast segments of critical infrastructure across allied and neutral states operate under a regime of "Rented Sovereignty" hosted on American backends.

### 🛡️ Vector C — Cyber & Cognitive Capability (`0.92` | Contribution: `0.2300`)
The U.S. commands peak offensive and signals intelligence capabilities (NSA, U.S. Cyber Command), reinforced by oversight of root DNS servers, global Root Certificate Authorities (Root CAs), and legal mechanisms for extraterritorial data extraction from transnational platforms (*US CLOUD Act*).

The sole vulnerability in this contour lies in the **semantic and cognitive plane**: the open architecture of commercial social platforms combined with deep domestic political polarization exposes the American information perimeter to external cognitive operations and algorithmic radicalization.

### 🏭 Vector I — Physical & Energetic Infrastructure (`0.70` | Contribution: `0.2100`)
The infrastructural contour represents the primary "Achilles' heel" of American hegemony. Despite dominating chip design (fabless giants such as Nvidia, AMD, Apple, and Qualcomm), transoceanic fiber-optic trunk lines, and orbital constellations (Starlink), the United States has lost domestic autonomy in leading-edge semiconductor manufacturing (process nodes below 5nm).

Total reliance on **TSMC** fabrication facilities in Taiwan creates a structural risk of "semiconductor strangulation." Even massive federal subsidies (*CHIPS and Science Act*) cannot rapidly close this gap due to specialized talent shortages and the high capital cost of building and operating onshore foundries.

### 📉 Vector V — Volatility & Shock Resilience (`0.20` | Contribution: `0.1200`)
A low volatility score (`0.20`) is anchored by the U.S. dollar's status as the global reserve currency, control over primary financial clearing rails (SWIFT, CHIPS), and high energy self-sufficiency (net hydrocarbon exporter status). The U.S. is virtually immune to conventional external financial sanctions. The primary risks in this vector are endogenous: rising sovereign debt servicing costs and institutional friction during political cycle transitions.

## 3. Geotechnological Dependency Matrix & Network Friction ($\Delta VSCI$)

Within the global dependency graph, the United States serves as the central hub—simultaneously projecting digital colonialism onto subordinate actors while remaining critically exposed to external chokepoints:

* **⬅️ INFLUENCES (Projection of Digital Colonialism):** **European Union**, **Japan**, **TSMC**, **ASML**.  
  Washington strictly dictates security protocols, export controls, and cloud architectures. Regarding the European Union ($S_{VSCI} \approx 0.51$), power asymmetry and extraterritorial mandates push the network friction metric $\Delta VSCI$ past the critical threshold $\Theta_{crit} = 0.25$, transforming the relationship into an infrastructural dependency of periphery upon metropole. Toward chokepoint corporations (Dutch ASML and Taiwanese TSMC), the U.S. weaponizes licensing controls (*FDPR* rules) to restrict strategic rivals.
* **➡️ DEPENDS ON (Existential Chokepoint):** **TSMC (Taiwan)**.  
  Critical supply-chain exposure in logic chips and AI accelerators compels Washington toward aggressive militarization of the Indo-Pacific to secure physical control over the island.
* **🔄 COMPETES WITH (Systemic Rivalry):** **China**.  
  An existential clash between two full-cycle poles for the status of global *Core of Power*, waged through "chip wars," AI compute cluster races, and competition for the alignment of *Managed Perimeter* states.
* **🤝 COOPERATES WITH (Institutional Alliances):** **United Kingdom**, **Japan** *(AUKUS / Five Eyes contours)*.  
  Deep coordination across intelligence alliances, synchronization of defense IT stacks, and joint containment of Chinese technological expansion.
