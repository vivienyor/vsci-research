## 2. Vector-by-Vector Structural Audit

### 🛡️ Vector C — Cyber & Cognitive Capability (`0.70` | Contribution: `0.1750`)
The strongest dimension of the European contour rests on institutional coordination (ENISA) and rigorous personal data protection regimes. However, legal safeguards collide with engineering reality: due to the extraterritorial reach of the *US CLOUD Act* and the lack of indigenous mass social platforms, Europe’s semantic space remains fully permeable to transatlantic algorithmic platforms.

### 🏭 Vector I — Physical & Energetic Infrastructure (`0.48` | Contribution: `0.1440`)
Despite hosting the global EUV lithography monopoly (**ASML**) in the Netherlands, bloc-wide infrastructural autonomy suffers from an acute deficit. The **Gaia-X** sovereign cloud initiative has effectively stalled: while data centers are physically located on European soil, their software architecture, hypervisors, and operational control belong to the American "Big Three" hyperscalers (AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud).

### 💻 Vector S — Technological & Logical Stack (`0.40` | Contribution: `0.1200`)
The European Union has ceded agency across application and system software tiers. Public administration, heavy industry, and financial institutions remain deeply locked into Microsoft, Oracle, and Google ecosystems. Promising European AI developers (France’s *Mistral AI*, Germany’s *Aleph Alpha*) are constrained by heavy regulatory overhead and lack compute clusters comparable to U.S. hyperscalers.

### 📉 Vector V — Volatility & Shock Resilience (`0.28` | Contribution: `0.1080`)
A low volatility score is anchored by the euro’s role as the world’s second reserve currency and the financial backstop of the European Central Bank (ECB). The primary source of instability is resource-driven: the loss of low-cost pipeline hydrocarbons has elevated electricity prices, directly impeding the deployment of energy-intensive AI compute clusters within the EU.

## 3. Geotechnological Dependency Matrix & Network Friction ($\Delta VSCI$)

* **⬅️ INFLUENCES:** **Global Markets via the "Brussels Effect"**.  
  The EU exports legal compliance standards and regulatory penalties, compelling multinational corporations to adapt to European rules, yet lacks a proprietary technological stack for hard digital projection.
* **➡️ DEPENDS ON (Systemic Vassalage):** **United States**, **AWS**, **Microsoft**, **Google**, **TSMC**.  
  The capability differential between the U.S. (`0.854`) and the EU (`0.547`) stands at `0.307`. Combined with low legal compatibility stemming from jurisdictional clashes, the network friction metric $\Delta VSCI$ breaches the critical threshold $\Theta_{crit} = 0.25$, locking the contour into infrastructural subordination.
* **🔄 COMPETES WITH:** **China**.  
  Trade and regulatory friction across electric vehicles, green technologies, and 5G/6G telecommunications standards.
* **🤝 COOPERATES WITH:** **United States**.  
  Deep integration into the transatlantic security architecture under Washington’s strategic and technological patronage.
